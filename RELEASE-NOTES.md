# Hamster Video 0.1.0 beta

An initial community preview for Intel Macs running macOS 15 or later.

- Download video links with selectable quality through 2160p when available.
- Extract MP3 audio, queue links, pause and resume.
- Choose Automatic, Light or Dark appearance.
- Keep local preferences and download history.

The build is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized. Website support depends on yt-dlp. Full 4K transfer, playlist completion and live subtitle retrieval have not yet been covered by release tests. See README.md for installation instructions and current limitations.
