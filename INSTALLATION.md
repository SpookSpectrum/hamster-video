# Installing Hamster Video 1.0.0

Requires macOS 15 or later on an Intel or Apple Silicon Mac.

1. Quit any older copy of Hamster Video.
2. Open `HamsterVideo-1.0.0-Universal.dmg`.
3. Drag **Hamster Video** on the left to **Applications** on the right. If updating an existing installation, choose Replace when Finder asks.
4. Eject the Hamster Video disk image and open the app from Applications.

This application is **ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized**. No paid Apple Developer account was used. macOS may block the first launch because Apple cannot verify its developer or check it for malicious software.

If you trust this download and macOS blocks it, attempt to open it once, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway** for Hamster Video. Confirm the app name before opening it. This is an app-specific exception; there is no need to disable Gatekeeper globally.

Apple explains the process in [Safely open apps on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

The installer deliberately contains no README, source archive, test reports or development files. This installation guide stays alongside the DMG.
